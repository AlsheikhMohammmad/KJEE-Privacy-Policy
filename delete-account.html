Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for the KJuffali Elevators mobile application, published by Khalid Juffali Elevators & Escalators Co.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to m.alsheikh@kjuffali.com with the subject line “Account deletion request – KJuffali Elevators”.
  2. Include the username you use to sign in to the app, and the email address or phone number registered on the account, so we can verify the request comes from the account holder.
  3. We will contact you using the details on the account to confirm the request.
  4. Once confirmed, we delete the account and complete the deletion described below within 30 days, and email you when it is done.

There is no charge for this request. You may withdraw it at any time before deletion is carried out. If we cannot verify that you own the account, we will not delete it.

What is deleted

When your deletion request is completed, we permanently delete:

  • Your account and login credentials (username and password hash)
  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your account type, company name, and commercial registration (CR) number
  • Your role and approval status
  • Your push notification tokens, which stops all notifications to your devices
  • The link between your account and any project, so remaining service records can no longer be traced back to you

What is kept, and for how long

Some records are maintenance and safety documentation for physical elevator and escalator equipment. We are required to retain these under our maintenance contracts and under applicable safety regulations, so they are not deleted with your account. They are separated from your identity as described above.

Data Kept? Retention period
Account details, contact information, credentials, push tokens Deleted Removed within 30 days of a confirmed request
Project, equipment, and contract records for serviced buildings Kept, unlinked from your account Life of the maintenance contract, plus any period required by law
Appointment records, safety checklists, and service history Kept, unlinked from your account Life of the maintenance contract, plus any period required by law
Service photos and completion signatures attached to past visits Kept as proof of work performed Life of the maintenance contract, plus any period required by law
Server security and access logs Kept Up to 90 days, then overwritten

If you want a service record removed as well as your account, say so in your email. We will remove anything we are not contractually or legally required to keep, and we will tell you specifically what we must retain and why.
